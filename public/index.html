About Guideon

Guideon is Oman's leading platform connecting travellers with local tour guides and tour companies. The majority of our guides — and every registered tour company — are licensed by the Ministry of Heritage & Tourism, clearly marked with a Verified badge. Founded in 2026, we make authentic Omani experiences accessible to everyone.

Every profile is reviewed by our team. The majority of our active guides — and all registered tour companies — hold a Ministry of Heritage & Tourism licence and carry a clear Verified badge, so you can book with confidence.
