About Guideon

Guideon is Oman's leading platform connecting travellers with Ministry-licensed local guides. Founded in 2025, we make authentic Omani experiences accessible to everyone — from ancient forts to desert dunes.

Every guide on our platform is personally verified, licensed by the Ministry of Heritage & Tourism, and committed to delivering genuine, memorable journeys across the Sultanate.
