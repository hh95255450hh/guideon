About Guideon

Oman's trusted marketplace connecting travellers with licensed local tour guides and registered tourism companies across the Sultanate.

Who we are

Guideon is an online platform that makes it simple to discover, compare, and book authentic guided experiences across Oman — from the forts of Nizwa and the fjords of Musandam to the dunes of the Wahiba Sands and the frankincense coast of Dhofar.

We connect tourists directly with local experts: independent tour guides and licensed tourism companies who know Oman intimately. Every booking, message, and payment happens securely on the platform.

Our mission

To put Oman's local guides at the heart of every traveller's journey — giving visitors authentic, safe, and well-organised experiences while helping Omani guides and tourism businesses grow a sustainable income.

What makes us different

من نحن
