Cancellation & Refund Policy

Clear rules so both travelers and guides know exactly where they stand.

100%

7+ days before

Full refund, no questions asked.

50%

3–7 days before

Half refunded to cover guide preparation.

0%

Less than 72 hours

No refund — the slot is reserved.

Free changes

You can change the date once at no cost, up to 48 hours before the tour starts, subject to guide availability.

Weather & safety cancellations

If the guide cancels for safety reasons (flash floods, sandstorm, road closure) you get a full refund — or we reschedule at no extra cost.

No-show

If you don't show up at the agreed meeting point and don't reply to the guide within 30 minutes, the booking is treated as a no-show with no refund.

How to cancel

  • Go to your dashboard → My Bookings
  • Click 'Cancel' on the booking
  • Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days to the original payment method

Custom packages & multi-day tours

For trips over 3 days or packages with hotel/transport components, a stricter policy may apply — these terms are shown on the booking page before payment.
