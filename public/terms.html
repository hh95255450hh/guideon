Tour companies (100% verified): All tour companies listed on Guideon are registered businesses that hold a current Ministry of Heritage & Tourism (MoHT) licence to operate as a tour operator in the Sultanate of Oman. Their commercial registration and MoHT licence numbers are collected at signup and reviewed by our team before their listing goes live.

Individual guides — "Verified" badge: A guide displays a ✓ Verified badge on their profile only after they have submitted to Guideon a Ministry of Heritage & Tourism licence number which our team has reviewed at the time of submission. The Verified badge is the only representation Guideon makes regarding an individual guide's licensing status. The majority of active guides on the platform are verified.

Non-verified individual guides: Guides without the Verified badge are listed as independent service providers. Guideon makes no representation regarding their licensing, qualifications, insurance, or fitness for any particular tour. Tourists are advised to confirm a guide's current MoHT licence directly with the guide or the Ministry before any high-risk activity (mountain trekking, water sports, etc.).

No employment, no agency: No guide on Guideon — verified or not — is an employee, agent, partner, or representative of Guideon. We do not vouch for individual conduct, language fluency, safety practices, equipment quality, or compliance with any specific law beyond presentation of a licence number.

4. Bookings, Cancellations & Refunds

Bookings: A booking is confirmed only after the guide accepts it. Guideon may auto-cancel bookings unaccepted within 24 hours.

Free cancellation: Cancellations made more than 48 hours before the tour start receive a full refund (less any payment-processor fees).

Late cancellation: Within 48 hours — 50% of tour cost is non-refundable. Within 24 hours — no refund.

No-show: No refund.

Guide-initiated cancellation: Full refund + Guideon credit equal to 10% of tour cost for re-booking.

Force majeure: Weather, government restrictions, or other events beyond control — full refund.
