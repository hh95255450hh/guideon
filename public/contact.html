❓ Common Questions About Our Emails

Quick answers about how email and communication work on Guideon.

Why didn't I receive my booking confirmation email?▼

Check your spam/junk folder first — emails from admin@guideon.om can occasionally land there. If still missing after 10 minutes, log in and check your dashboard, or contact admin@guideon.om.

Can I reply to automated emails?▼

Yes! When you click "Reply" on any Guideon email, your response is automatically routed to admin@guideon.om and reaches our support team within seconds.

How do I stop receiving marketing emails?▼

Every newsletter has an "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom. You can also log in and adjust your notification preferences in your account settings. Booking-related emails (confirmations, refunds) cannot be disabled for security reasons.

Are my messages and data secure?▼

Absolutely. All emails use TLS encryption in transit, and we authenticate our domain with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC to prevent spoofing. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

What languages do you support?▼

All our emails and support channels are fully bilingual (English & Arabic). You'll receive both versions in the same email, and our team replies in your preferred language.

How fast do you reply?▼

WhatsApp: under 1 hour. Email: under 24 hours (usually 2–4 hours during Oman business hours). Urgent booking issues are prioritized and replied to within 30 minutes during business hours.
