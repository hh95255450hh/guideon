## Full description — EN
Discover, book, and message licensed local tour guides and registered tourism
companies across the Sultanate of Oman. Guideon connects travellers with
trusted, verified guides for authentic experiences — from Muscat's forts to the
dunes of the Empty Quarter and the fjords of Musandam.

• Browse verified guides and tourism companies
• Filter by city, language, and specialty
• Secure booking with transparent pricing (no subscriptions)
• In-app messaging with your guide
• Manage your trips and bookings in one place

Guideon is operated by Vision for Digital Thought, a registered Omani company.
